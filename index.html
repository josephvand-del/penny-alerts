      What this app does NOT do

      • It never checks a store’s price by itself. No Walmart or retailer site is ever called automatically.
      • It cannot confirm a penny at a specific store. Only a scan in the store’s app — and the register — can do that.
      • Dollar General has no remote price check at all, paid or free. DG pennies are list → drive → scan in the DG app.
      • Background push is live once setup is done (see README): enable alerts and new deals push to this phone, even when the app is closed. Until the setup is done, alerts are checked when you open the app.
